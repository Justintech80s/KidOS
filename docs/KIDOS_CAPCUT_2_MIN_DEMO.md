# KidOS — 2-Minute CapCut Demo Production Package

**Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, ~2:00
**Narration:** CapCut AI text-to-speech; calm professional voice; ~0.95× speed
**Captions:** Auto captions, sentence case
**Music:** Subtle technology/education instrumental, mixed well below narration

> Accuracy rule: demonstrate implemented behavior as implemented. For roadmap capabilities, use “designed to” or “being developed to”; do not imply independent security certification.

## CapCut build settings

1. Create a 16:9 1080p project.
2. Import the two KidOS PNGs and KidOS_Demo_iPhone.mp4.
3. Assemble clips according to the timeline above.
4. Open Text → Text to speech and paste each narration section separately.
5. Select one calm, professional English AI voice and keep it consistent.
6. Start around 0.95× narration speed; adjust scene duration to preserve natural pauses.
7. Generate Auto Captions from the finished narration.
8. Keep background music low enough that every narrated word remains clear.
9. Use restrained fades/slides rather than rapid social-media effects.
10. Export H.264 MP4, 1920×1080.

## Final QA checklist

- Runtime approximately 1:55–2:05.
- Narration is intelligible on phone speakers.
- Captions match narration.
- No claim says KidOS is independently certified unless certification exists.
- Roadmap capabilities are presented as designs/in-development.
- No personal child data appears.
- Final partnership screen remains visible long enough to read.
